FashionForward Global — Our Planet, Our Promise: Sustainability Vision 2024
Sector: Retail/Fashion | Fiscal Year: 2024

A MESSAGE FROM OUR CEO
At FashionForward, we believe that style and sustainability are not just compatible — they are inseparable. We are deeply committed to being a force for good on our planet. Our customers share our values, and together we are building a more beautiful, more sustainable world. Climate action is at the heart of everything we do.

OUR BOLD COMMITMENT
FashionForward is proud to be a carbon-neutral brand. We have achieved carbon neutrality for our retail operations in 2022 and have maintained this status through 2023. We are committed to being a net-positive company by 2050, leaving the planet better than we found it.

WHAT CARBON NEUTRAL MEANS FOR US
Our carbon neutral status is achieved through a rigorous, comprehensive approach:
- We measure the emissions from our offices, stores, and distribution centers
- We implement energy efficiency initiatives wherever possible
- We offset 100% of remaining emissions through certified carbon credits

In 2023, we offset 185,000 tCO2e through a portfolio of projects including wind energy in India, cookstove distribution in Kenya, and rainforest protection in Brazil.

OUR SUSTAINABLE COLLECTIONS
We are proud that our Eco-Line collection uses 40% recycled materials. By 2025, we commit to ensuring 50% of our materials are sustainably sourced. We have also launched our GarmentReturn program, which allows customers to return used clothes for recycling — we recycled 2 million garments in 2023!

SUPPLY CHAIN
Fashion is global, and our supply chain spans 47 countries. We work with over 2,000 suppliers who share our commitment to sustainability. We have issued our Supplier Code of Conduct, which includes environmental guidelines. We are working with our key suppliers to improve environmental practices.

We are proud that 15% of our Tier 1 suppliers have completed our sustainability questionnaire in 2023. We are aiming for 30% by 2026.

ABOUT OUR EMISSIONS
Our direct operations (stores, offices, owned logistics) represent only a small fraction of our overall footprint. We are transparent that the majority of our fashion's environmental impact occurs in the production and use phases — but these emissions are largely outside of our direct control as a retailer.

Our retail operations carbon footprint in 2023: 185,000 tCO2e (100% offset).

We have not separately quantified our Scope 3 manufacturing emissions, as these depend on many factors beyond our influence. We believe our sustainable sourcing policies are the most effective lever we have in this area.

CLIMATE GOALS
- Carbon neutral retail operations: Achieved 2022 ✓
- 50% sustainable materials by 2025
- Net-positive company by 2050
- Partner with suppliers to improve sustainability

We believe that through our offset program, our sustainable collections, and our supplier partnerships, we are making a meaningful contribution to fighting climate change.

AWARDS AND RECOGNITION
We are proud to have been recognized by:
- FashionIndex Sustainability Award 2023 (Finalist)

- Included in the Sustainable Fashion Magazine "Top 50 Green Brands" list

THIRD-PARTY VERIFICATION
Our offset portfolio is managed by EcoCredits International, a leading carbon offset broker. All offsets are certified under Verified Carbon Standard (VCS) or Gold Standard.

CONCLUSION
FashionForward is committed to making fashion that doesn't cost the Earth. We invite our customers, suppliers, and stakeholders to join us on this journey. Together, we can make style sustainable.
