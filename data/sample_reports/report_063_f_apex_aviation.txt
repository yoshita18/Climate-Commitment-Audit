Apex Aviation — Sustainability Vision Document 2024
Sector: Transport & Logistics | Region: North America

OUR SUSTAINABILITY VISION
We are committed to a sustainable future and take our environmental responsibilities seriously.
We aspire to become carbon neutral as quickly as possible, with a long-term vision of
achieving net-zero by 2090 as technology and market conditions allow.

Our absolute emissions have grown by 19% since 2015, reflecting our
business expansion. On an intensity basis, we have improved efficiency.

We offset our executive team's travel emissions, demonstrating our commitment.

ENVIRONMENTAL HIGHLIGHTS
- Recycled 50% of office waste
- Switched to eco-friendly cleaning products
- Organised a company tree-planting day

GOVERNANCE
We are developing our governance framework for sustainability.

DISCLAIMER
Sustainability commitments are aspirational and subject to business conditions.
