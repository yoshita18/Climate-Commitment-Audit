PetroMax Industries — Climate Progress Report 2024
Sector: Oil & Gas | Fiscal Year: 2024

EXECUTIVE STATEMENT ON CLIMATE
PetroMax Industries recognizes the challenge of climate change and has made significant progress in reducing our environmental impact. We are proud to report that we have achieved a 30% reduction in emissions intensity since 2005, demonstrating our commitment to continuous improvement.

OUR CLIMATE COMMITMENTS
PetroMax is committed to:
- Achieving net-zero emissions by 2070
- Reducing emissions intensity by 50% by 2050 compared to our 2005 baseline
- Investing $500M in clean energy technologies by 2030
- Achieving carbon neutrality in our upstream operations by 2035

EMISSIONS PERFORMANCE
We are proud of our emissions intensity improvements:
- 2005: 42 kgCO2e per barrel of oil equivalent (BOE)
- 2023: 29.4 kgCO2e per BOE
- Improvement: 30% intensity reduction over 18 years

Absolute emissions data:
- 2005 absolute Scope 1+2: 18.2 MtCO2e
- 2023 absolute Scope 1+2: 22.8 MtCO2e
- Change in absolute emissions: +25% (driven by production growth)

Scope 3 emissions: We do not report Scope 3 emissions as industry-standard methodologies for our sector continue to evolve. The combustion of our products by customers is beyond our operational control.

CLEAN ENERGY INVESTMENTS
We have committed $500M to clean energy through 2030. Our investments to date include:
- Carbon capture and storage (CCS) research: $180M committed
- Blue hydrogen feasibility studies: $45M
- Offset forestry projects: $120M
- Renewable energy for our own operations: $55M
- Other sustainability initiatives: $100M

We believe CCS technology will allow us to continue oil and gas production while meeting climate goals. We are a founding member of the Carbon Capture Coalition.

THE ROLE OF OIL AND GAS IN THE ENERGY TRANSITION
Reliable, affordable energy is the foundation of economic development. Natural gas plays a critical bridging role in the energy transition, and we are proud to supply this essential resource. According to our analysis, global oil demand will not peak until the mid-2030s, and investment in new supply is essential for energy security.

We believe the IPCC acknowledges the role of carbon capture in enabling continued fossil fuel use in the transition period, which validates our business model and long-term investment strategy.

METHANE MANAGEMENT
Methane emissions are a priority. We have achieved a methane intensity of 0.31% of natural gas production. We are working to achieve 0.20% by 2028.

BIODIVERSITY AND NATURE
We have offset 850,000 tCO2e through conservation projects protecting 2.1 million acres of forest in South America. These projects are central to our 2035 carbon neutrality goal for upstream operations.

WHAT "CARBON NEUTRALITY" MEANS FOR OUR UPSTREAM
Our upstream operations will be "carbon neutral" by 2035 by combining:
1. Emissions intensity improvements
2. CCS deployment at two major facilities (projected capacity: 1.5 MtCO2e/year by 2033)
3. Nature-based offset portfolio (targeting 5 MtCO2e/year)

Current upstream absolute emissions: approximately 15 MtCO2e/year. Achieving carbon neutrality will require approximately 12 MtCO2e/year of offsets and CCS by 2035 — more than 80% of our offset strategy.

ALIGNMENT WITH INTERNATIONAL FRAMEWORKS
We believe our targets are consistent with the Paris Agreement's goal of keeping warming well below 2°C. Our 2070 net-zero target reflects the realistic timescale for our sector given the complexity of decarbonizing hard-to-abate operations. We note that the IPCC's 1.5°C pathway involves significant assumptions about unproven technologies, and we believe prudent planning requires a more conservative timeline.

INVESTOR INFORMATION
We believe our climate strategy balances our environmental responsibilities with our obligation to deliver competitive returns to shareholders. We do not believe near-term aggressive decarbonization is in the interest of our stakeholders.
