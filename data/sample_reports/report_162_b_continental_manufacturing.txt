Continental Manufacturing — Climate Progress Report 2023
Sector: Manufacturing | Region: United Kingdom

OUR CLIMATE TARGETS
We have set the following targets, pending SBTi validation:
- Scope 1 and 2: 36% absolute reduction by 2030 vs 2018
- Scope 3: 25% absolute reduction by 2030 vs 2018
- Net-zero emissions by 2048

BASELINE (2018)
Scope 1: 745,270 tCO2e

PROGRESS TO DATE
