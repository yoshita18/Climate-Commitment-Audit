Alpine Manufacturing — Climate Progress Report 2024
Sector: Manufacturing | Region: Latin America

OUR CLIMATE TARGETS
We have set the following targets, pending SBTi validation:
- Scope 1 and 2: 45% absolute reduction by 2030 vs 2019
- Scope 3: 26% absolute reduction by 2030 vs 2019
- Net-zero emissions by 2047

BASELINE (2019)
Scope 1: 926,918 tCO2e
Scope 2 (market-based): 261,118 tCO2e
Scope 3: 879,881 tCO2e

PROGRESS TO DATE
23% Scope 1+2 reduction achieved. On track for 2030 milestones.
Renewable electricity: 56% of consumption.
Offsetting: <10% of total reductions from offsets; direct cuts prioritised.

KEY ACTIONS
- Procured 52% renewable electricity in 2023 (PPAs + RECs)
- Launched 4 energy efficiency programmes across manufacturing
- Internal carbon price: $60/tCO2e
- Supplier engagement programme: 43% of Tier 1 suppliers surveyed
CDP Score: B | SBTi Status: Committed

GOVERNANCE
Board-level oversight. Executive bonus 10% linked to sustainability KPIs. CDP: B.

DISCLAIMER
Targets subject to annual review and aligned with Paris Agreement.
