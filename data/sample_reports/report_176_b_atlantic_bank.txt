Atlantic Bank — Climate Progress Report 2024
Sector: Financial Services | Region: North America

OUR CLIMATE TARGETS
We have set the following targets, pending SBTi validation:
- Scope 1 and 2: 42% absolute reduction by 2030 vs 2018
- Scope 3: 35% absolute reduction by 2030 vs 2018
- Net-zero emissions by 2050

BASELINE (2018)
Scope 1: 602,351 tCO2e
Scope 2 (market-based): 142,190 tCO2e
Scope 3: 2,295,069 tCO2e

PROGRESS TO DATE
27% Scope 1+2 reduction achieved. On track for 2030 milestones.
Renewable electricity: 62% of consumption.
Offsetting: <12% of total reductions from offsets; direct cuts prioritised.

KEY ACTIONS
- Procured 67% renewable electricity in 2023 (PPAs + RECs)
- Launched 4 energy efficiency programmes across manufacturing
- Internal carbon price: $48/tCO2e
- Supplier engagement programme: 33% of Tier 1 suppliers surveyed

GOVERNANCE
Board-level oversight. Executive bonus 11% linked to sustainability KPIs. CDP: B.
