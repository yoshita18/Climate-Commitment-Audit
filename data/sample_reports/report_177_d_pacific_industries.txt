Pacific Industries — Environmental Commitment Statement 2024
Sector: Manufacturing | Region: North America

OUR CLIMATE AMBITION
We aspire to achieve carbon neutrality in our operations by 2066.

We have reduced our emissions intensity by 22% since 2010, demonstrating
our long-term commitment to environmental stewardship.

We do not currently report Scope 3 emissions as industry methodologies continue to evolve.

Our carbon neutrality commitment will be achieved primarily through:
- Operational efficiency improvements (25% of expected reductions)
- High-quality carbon credits (75% of expected reductions)

ENVIRONMENTAL PROGRAMMES
- LED lighting retrofit completed at 33% of facilities
- Renewable energy PPA signed for one major facility
- Employee engagement: Green Team launched in 8 offices

GOVERNANCE
Sustainability working group established. Annual internal reporting.

DISCLAIMER
All figures are unaudited management estimates.
