Atlantic Retail — Science-Aligned Net-Zero Transition Plan 2022
Sector: Retail & Consumer Goods | Region: Latin America

OUR SCIENCE-BASED CLIMATE TARGETS (SBTi VALIDATED)
Near-term (2030): Reduce absolute Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 55% versus 2020 baseline.
Near-term (2030): Reduce absolute Scope 3 emissions by 43% versus 2020 baseline.
Long-term (2050): Net-zero across Scopes 1, 2, and 3 by 2049.
Interim (2035):   78% absolute reduction in Scope 1 and 2.

All targets independently validated by SBTi in 2023 and aligned with the 1.5°C pathway
as defined in IPCC AR6. Targets cover 100% of our operational footprint and all material
Scope 3 categories (purchased goods, use of sold products, end-of-life treatment).

BASELINE EMISSIONS (2020)
Scope 1: 728,133 tCO2e

Scope 3 (15 categories): 754,056 tCO2e
Scope 3 share of total: 90%

CURRENT PROGRESS
Scope 1+2 reduction vs baseline: 31%
Scope 3 reduction vs baseline: 22%

Third-party assurance: Bureau Veritas (reasonable assurance, Scope 1+2; limited, Scope 3)

KEY DECARBONISATION ACTIONS
1. Renewable Energy: 85% clean electricity via long-term PPAs; 100% by 2026.
2. Fleet Electrification: 70% of owned vehicles electric by 2028.
3. Supply Chain: Requiring all Tier 1 suppliers (80% of spend) to set SBTi targets by 2026.
4. Carbon Removal: $71M invested in high-quality CDR (DAC + nature-based) by 2030.
5. Internal Carbon Price: $118/tCO2e applied to all capital decisions >$1M.
