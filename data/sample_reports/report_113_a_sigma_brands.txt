Sigma Brands — Science-Aligned Net-Zero Transition Plan 2022
Sector: Retail & Consumer Goods | Region: Asia-Pacific

OUR SCIENCE-BASED CLIMATE TARGETS (SBTi VALIDATED)
Near-term (2030): Reduce absolute Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 47% versus 2019 baseline.
Near-term (2030): Reduce absolute Scope 3 emissions by 40% versus 2019 baseline.
Long-term (2050): Net-zero across Scopes 1, 2, and 3 by 2046.
Interim (2035):   74% absolute reduction in Scope 1 and 2.

All targets independently validated by SBTi in 2023 and aligned with the 1.5°C pathway
as defined in IPCC AR6. Targets cover 100% of our operational footprint and all material
Scope 3 categories (purchased goods, use of sold products, end-of-life treatment).

KEY DECARBONISATION ACTIONS
1. Renewable Energy: 93% clean electricity via long-term PPAs; 100% by 2026.
2. Fleet Electrification: 75% of owned vehicles electric by 2028.
3. Supply Chain: Requiring all Tier 1 suppliers (80% of spend) to set SBTi targets by 2026.
4. Carbon Removal: $69M invested in high-quality CDR (DAC + nature-based) by 2030.
5. Internal Carbon Price: $111/tCO2e applied to all capital decisions >$1M.
