Crown Retail — Climate Progress Report 2022
Sector: Retail & Consumer Goods | Region: North America

OUR CLIMATE TARGETS
We have set the following targets, pending SBTi validation:
- Scope 1 and 2: 39% absolute reduction by 2030 vs 2019
- Scope 3: 33% absolute reduction by 2030 vs 2019
- Net-zero emissions by 2049

BASELINE (2019)
Scope 1: 211,579 tCO2e
Scope 2 (market-based): 186,471 tCO2e
Scope 3: 5,959,896 tCO2e

PROGRESS TO DATE
22% Scope 1+2 reduction achieved. On track for 2030 milestones.
Renewable electricity: 64% of consumption.
Offsetting: <11% of total reductions from offsets; direct cuts prioritised.

KEY ACTIONS
- Procured 54% renewable electricity in 2023 (PPAs + RECs)
- Launched 4 energy efficiency programmes across manufacturing
- Internal carbon price: $50/tCO2e
- Supplier engagement programme: 47% of Tier 1 suppliers surveyed
CDP Score: B | SBTi Status: Committed

GOVERNANCE
Board-level oversight. Executive bonus 8% linked to sustainability KPIs. CDP: B.

DISCLAIMER
Targets subject to annual review and aligned with Paris Agreement.
