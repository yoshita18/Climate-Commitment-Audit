Nexus Realty — Climate Progress Report 2022
Sector: Real Estate & Construction | Region: Asia-Pacific

OUR CLIMATE TARGETS
We have set the following targets, pending SBTi validation:
- Scope 1 and 2: 35% absolute reduction by 2030 vs 2019
- Scope 3: 30% absolute reduction by 2030 vs 2019
- Net-zero emissions by 2050

BASELINE (2019)
Scope 1: 781,533 tCO2e
Scope 2 (market-based): 205,788 tCO2e
Scope 3: 1,825,150 tCO2e

PROGRESS TO DATE
16% Scope 1+2 reduction achieved. On track for 2030 milestones.
Renewable electricity: 46% of consumption.
Offsetting: <12% of total reductions from offsets; direct cuts prioritised.

KEY ACTIONS
- Procured 65% renewable electricity in 2023 (PPAs + RECs)
- Launched 3 energy efficiency programmes across manufacturing
- Internal carbon price: $70/tCO2e
- Supplier engagement programme: 44% of Tier 1 suppliers surveyed
CDP Score: B | SBTi Status: Committed

GOVERNANCE
Board-level oversight. Executive bonus 8% linked to sustainability KPIs. CDP: B.

DISCLAIMER
Targets subject to annual review and aligned with Paris Agreement.
