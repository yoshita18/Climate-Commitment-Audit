Advanced SteelWorks International — Net-Zero Transition Plan 2024
Sector: Industry (Steel) | Fiscal Year: 2024

EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Advanced SteelWorks International (ASI) produces 12 million tonnes of steel annually across 8 facilities in 5 countries. Steel production currently accounts for approximately 7-9% of global CO2 emissions. We recognize we are a hard-to-abate sector and have developed the most rigorous, science-based decarbonization roadmap possible.

Our validated SBTi commitments:
- Scope 1+2: 42% absolute reduction by 2030 (2019 baseline: 28.4 MtCO2e)
- Scope 3: 25% absolute reduction by 2030 (2019 baseline: 4.1 MtCO2e)
- Net-zero across all scopes by 2050

CURRENT EMISSIONS PROFILE
Total 2023 Emissions:
- Scope 1 (direct, primarily blast furnace): 24.8 MtCO2e
- Scope 2 (purchased electricity, market-based): 2.1 MtCO2e
- Scope 3 Cat 1 (iron ore, coal, purchased goods): 3.6 MtCO2e
- Scope 3 Cat 11 (processing of sold products): 0.6 MtCO2e
Total: 31.1 MtCO2e

Progress vs 2019 baseline:
- Scope 1+2 absolute reduction: 8.7% (on track for 2025 milestone of 15%)
- Carbon intensity: 2.59 tCO2e/tonne steel (vs 2.81 in 2019, -8%)

PRIMARY DECARBONIZATION LEVERS

1. HYDROGEN-BASED DIRECT REDUCTION (H-DRI)
This is our core long-term pathway. We have:
- Completed a full-scale H-DRI pilot at our Linz facility (capacity: 100,000 t/year)
- Signed an offtake agreement for 80,000 tonnes/year of green hydrogen by 2027
- Committed €1.2B to convert our Linz and Hamburg blast furnaces to H-DRI by 2030
- Projected H-DRI capacity by 2030: 3.5 million tonnes/year (29% of total production)

H-DRI pathway requires green hydrogen at scale and below €3/kg. We are co-funding an electrolyzer project targeting 1 GW capacity by 2028.

2. ELECTRIC ARC FURNACE (EAF) EXPANSION
EAF with scrap steel produces approximately 0.4 tCO2e/tonne vs 2.1 tCO2e/tonne for blast furnace.
- 2023: 22% of production via EAF (industry average: ~30%)
- 2030 target: 45% of production via EAF
- 2040 target: 65% of production via EAF
- Requires scrap availability and renewable electricity access

3. CARBON CAPTURE, UTILIZATION AND STORAGE (CCUS)
For remaining blast furnace operations through 2035, we are deploying:
- Top gas recycling blast furnace (TGR-BF) at Rotterdam facility (pilot 2025)
- CCUS retrofit at Rotterdam facility by 2030 (capture capacity: 2.5 MtCO2e/year)

CCUS is a bridging technology only; we do not assume CCUS beyond 2040 in our base case.

4. RENEWABLE ELECTRICITY
- 2023: 31% renewable electricity (from PPAs and own wind)
- 2030 target: 75% renewable electricity
- Signed 15-year PPAs totaling 2.8 TWh/year, operational by 2026

CAPITAL INVESTMENT PLAN
Total committed CapEx for decarbonization: €4.8B through 2035
- H-DRI conversion: €2.1B
- EAF expansion: €1.3B
- CCUS at Rotterdam: €0.8B
- Renewable energy: €0.6B

Our internal carbon price: €110/tCO2e (above EU ETS at €65/tCO2e in 2024)

RESIDUAL EMISSIONS AND CDR
By 2050, we project residual emissions of approximately 1.2 MtCO2e/year from processes that cannot be fully abated with current technology. We will address these through:
- High-quality direct air capture contracts (committed 500,000 tCO2e/year from 2045)
- Nature-based solutions with permanence guarantees: 700,000 tCO2e/year

Offsets represent less than 4% of our current emissions and will remain below 10% of residual emissions in 2050.

JUST TRANSITION
Decarbonization will affect approximately 8,400 workers in fossil fuel-dependent roles. We have:
- Committed €180M to a Just Transition Fund for retraining and regional economic development
- Partnered with 6 universities for steel technology education programs
- Engaged works councils in all facilities as full partners in the transition plan

INDUSTRY ENGAGEMENT
We are a founding member of ResponsibleSteel, Mission Possible Partnership (steel), and Hydrogen Europe. We are sharing our H-DRI technology roadmap under open-source licensing to accelerate sector decarbonization.

PERFORMANCE GOVERNANCE
- Net-zero targets overseen by Board; quarterly emissions reporting to ExCo
- CEO and CFO variable pay tied 20% to emissions reduction milestones
- Annual independent verification by DNV (limited assurance, Scopes 1+2; reasonable assurance for key metrics)
- Reporting per GHG Protocol, TCFD, and EU Taxonomy alignment assessment

RISKS AND SCENARIO ANALYSIS
We model three scenarios:
- 1.5°C Orderly (Net Zero by 2050): Full deployment of H-DRI by 2035, green H2 at €2/kg
- 2°C Delayed (Current Policies + late action): Slower H-DRI adoption, higher carbon cost exposure
- Physical Risk (3°C): Significant water stress at 3 of our facilities by 2040

Our strategy is optimized for the 1.5°C scenario. We are transparent that the 2°C delayed scenario would require significant additional investment and may result in stranded assets.
