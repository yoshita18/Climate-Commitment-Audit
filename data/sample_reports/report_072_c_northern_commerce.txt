Northern Commerce — Sustainability Report 2024
Sector: Retail & Consumer Goods | Region: United Kingdom

OUR SUSTAINABILITY GOALS
We are committed to achieving net-zero emissions by 2050 across our direct operations.

Our near-term goal is to reduce our carbon intensity by 38% per unit of revenue
by 2030, compared to our 2019 baseline.

Regarding Scope 3: We are developing a methodology to quantify and set targets for our
value chain emissions. We plan to publish Scope 3 targets by 2026.

OFFSET STRATEGY
We will use a combination of operational improvements and high-quality carbon credits
to achieve carbon neutrality in our headquarters operations by 2028.
We currently offset 45% of our Scope 1+2 emissions through verified projects.

SUSTAINABILITY INITIATIVES
- Energy efficiency: $28M invested in equipment upgrades since 2020
- Renewable energy: 41% of electricity from renewables
- Tree planting: 1,339,809 trees planted in partnership with NGOs
CDP Score: C

GOVERNANCE
Chief Sustainability Officer reports to CFO. Sustainability included in executive scorecard.

DISCLAIMER
Targets reflect our current best estimates and are subject to revision.
